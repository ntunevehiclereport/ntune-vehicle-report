What you get

One report. Every item linked to its source.

The goal is a report that's quick, convenient and reliable to use: every item is researched for your specific vehicle and links to its source, so you can check it yourself. If something can't be backed up for your vehicle, it's left out.

The main event

Your codes and symptoms, converted

A plain-language read of the trouble codes you enter (like P0300 or C1234), the symptoms you describe, and any diagnostic scan file you upload: what each code means, what commonly causes it, and next steps listed easiest first.

Safety recalls

Recalls listed by NHTSA for your year, make and model: who each one covers, the risk, and what to do. We point you to the official VIN lookup to confirm whether your vehicle is included.

Common areas owners report

The areas that come up most in owner complaints filed with NHTSA for your model year, so you know what's worth keeping an eye on.

Your owner's manual

A link to a free online copy of your owner's manual when one can be found, and a plain note when one can't.

Maintenance, known issues and care tips

Maintenance for your model, service bulletins when they're found and checked for your vehicle, and care tips, each with its source.

Near you

Mobile mechanics, parts stores and shops

Places near the city or ZIP you enter, looked up when your report is written. We only ask for a city or ZIP, never a street address. Listings are not ads or paid placements.

Free extra

Check engine light first-steps checklist

A one-page PDF that comes with your report: what to check first when the light comes on, in plain language, easiest first, and when to stop driving.

How it works

Four steps, no phone calls.

Answer a few short questions on one screen at a time. The more you give us, especially a scan from your shop, the more useful your report can be.

Start your free report

  1. Tell us about your vehicle

    Year, make and model, plus your city or ZIP. The VIN is optional.

  2. Add codes and symptoms

    Type any trouble codes and describe what the vehicle is doing: noises, warning lights, when it happens.

  3. Upload your shop's scan

    For best results, attach the diagnostic scan results from your local repair shop (PDF or a photo works).

  4. Get your report by email

    Your report arrives by email. Read it, check the sources, and decide your next move.

FAQs

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes. Reports are free while we test with a small group. There's no account, no subscription and nothing you're required to buy.

Do I need a VIN or a scan tool?

No. The VIN and mileage are optional, and you can send codes, a description of what the vehicle is doing, a scan file, or any mix. For best results, upload the diagnostic scan results from your local auto repair shop.

Is a report a diagnosis?

No. A report is general information. It's not a vehicle inspection, not a replacement for a good diagnostic tool, and not a substitute for an in-person diagnosis by a qualified technician. See the disclaimer.

How long does a report take?

There's no promised turnaround. This is a test version and reports are prepared one at a time, so timing can vary. Your report arrives by email.

What do you do with my information?

We use what you send only to prepare and email your report. We only ask for a city or ZIP, never a street address, and no phone number or payment. Your information is never sold to third parties or used to target ads.

Can I put my shop's name on the report?

Yes. The form has an optional "Want your company or name on the header?" question. Leave it blank for the standard NTune Vehicle Report header.

Got a code or a strange noise?

Reports are free during testing. All you need is your vehicle and what it's doing.
