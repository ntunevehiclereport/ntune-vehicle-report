P0302  Cylinder 2 misfire detected Serious

The engine computer saw cylinder 2 fail to fire properly. This is a generic code, so it means the same thing on most vehicles; the cause still has to be found on this one.

Common causes Most often an ignition problem (the cylinder 2 coil, spark plug or plug boot). A fuel injector, fuel delivery, a large vacuum leak or an engine mechanical problem can also cause it.

Why it matters A misfire like this can damage the catalytic converter and the engine, so it's treated as serious. The owner's manual says a blinking check engine light on this Civic means a misfire was detected.

How your symptoms fit A rough idle and a flashing check engine light are both listed symptoms of this code. That's a match with the code, not a confirmation of the cause.

Sources: P0302 Cylinder 2 Misfire Detected (OBD-Codes.com); 2014 Civic owner's manual, check engine light comes on or blinks (p. 436)

Your next steps, easiest first

  1. If the light is blinking, stop driving. Safety Easy

    The manual says to park somewhere safe, away from anything flammable, and let the engine cool at least 10 minutes. If the light blinks again after restarting, it says to have the vehicle inspected and to drive no faster than 31 mph to get there. Owner's manual p. 436

  2. Check when the spark plugs were last done. Easy

    On this Civic, spark plug replacement is part of Maintenance Minder item 4. If your records don't show it, that's worth knowing before you buy parts. Owner's manual, Maintenance Minder items (p. 355-356)

  3. Look at the cylinder 2 coil, plug and boot. DIY with basic tools

    Look for oil, coolant or water on the coil, boot or plug, and check the coil's wiring and connector. Parts contaminated with liquid need to be cleaned or replaced. P0302 diagnosis steps (OBD-Codes.com)

  4. Rule out other codes first. Needs a scan

    If lean or fuel-supply codes show up with the misfire code, those get diagnosed first. A full scan from your shop shows every stored code and the conditions when this one set. P0302 diagnosis steps (OBD-Codes.com)

  5. Spark and injector tests. Involved

    Testing spark at the coil and plug, then the injector, pins down the cause. Ignition systems carry very high voltage, so if you're not set up for it, this step is best done at a shop. P0302 diagnosis steps (OBD-Codes.com)

NHTSA recall data here is by year, make and model, not by VIN. Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see if your vehicle is included. Recall repairs are free at a dealer.

Transmission software

Sep 15, 2015 · NHTSA #15V574000 · Honda recall JU2 (Civic)

Who it covers Certain 2014-2015 Civics built January 16, 2014 to November 6, 2014.

Risk Transmission software settings may damage the transmission drive pulley shaft. If it breaks, the vehicle may lose acceleration or the front wheels may lock up while driving, increasing the risk of a crash.

What to do Dealers update the transmission software, free of charge.

Source: NHTSA, 2014 Honda Civic · NHTSA recall data for this model

Tires

Mar 10, 2014 · NHTSA #14V109000 · Honda recall JD8

Who it covers Certain 2014 Civic LX vehicles built November 26, 2013 through January 21, 2014.

Risk During tire mounting at the factory, the tire bead may have been pinched and damaged. The tire could lose air, increasing the risk of a crash.

What to do Dealers inspect and replace any damaged tire, free of charge.

Source: NHTSA, 2014 Honda Civic · NHTSA recall data for this model

Maintenance for this model

Oil and filter: when the Maintenance Minder says

This Civic tracks oil life as a percentage. Once oil life drops below 15%, Maintenance Minder messages appear each time you turn the key to ON. The manual calls for Genuine Honda Motor Oil or premium-grade 0W-20 detergent oil with the API Certification Seal.

Sources: Owner's manual, Maintenance Minder (p. 353-354) · Owner's manual, recommended engine oil

What the Maintenance Minder numbers mean

Alongside the oil message, numbered items tell you what else is due: 1 rotate tires; 2 replace the air cleaner and dust and pollen filter, inspect the drive belt; 3 replace transmission fluid; 4 replace spark plugs and inspect valve clearance; 5 replace engine coolant.

Source: Owner's manual, Maintenance Minder items (p. 355-356)

Care tips for this model

Check tire pressure monthly, when cold

The manual says tires can lose 1 to 2 psi a month and can read 4 to 6 psi higher when hot. Check them cold, at least once a month and before long trips, and set them to the pressure on the label on the driver's doorjamb. If you adjust the pressure, the tire pressure monitor needs to be calibrated (see the manual).

Source: Owner's manual, checking tires

Fuel: 87 octane or higher

The manual warns that gasoline below 87 octane can damage the engine, and Honda endorses TOP TIER detergent gasoline where available.

Source: Owner's manual, fuel information (p. 343)

Check engine light on? Check the gas cap too

A loose or missing fuel cap can turn on the check engine light. The manual says to tighten the cap until it clicks at least once. (It won't cause a misfire code like the one above.)

Source: Owner's manual, check fuel cap message (p. 436-437)
