How reviews work

  • Reviews are checked before they appear on the site, so yours won't show right away.
  • We post reviews as written. We don't edit what you say, though we may leave out personal details like phone numbers or addresses.
  • We don't pay for reviews or offer anything in exchange for a good one.
  • Your email (if you give it) is never published. It's only used to follow up about your review.
