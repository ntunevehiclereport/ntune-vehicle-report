Disclaimer

Please read this before you request or rely on a report.

Draft for review · Last updated November 8, 2026

The short version

This is not a replacement for a good diagnostic tool. For best results, upload the diagnostic scan results from your local auto repair shop.

A report is general information. It is not a vehicle inspection and not a substitute for an in-person diagnosis by a qualified technician.

1. What a report is

A report is a plain-language explanation of the trouble codes, symptoms and scan files you send us, along with general information about your vehicle's year, make and model: safety recalls and owner complaints from public NHTSA databases, a link to an owner's manual when one can be found, maintenance and care tips, and nearby mobile mechanics, parts stores and shops. Each item links to the source it came from so you can check it yourself.

2. What a report is not

  • It is not a replacement for a good diagnostic tool. A report can only work with the information you give us. A proper scan tool, used on your vehicle, can see live data and details that codes and descriptions alone can't.
  • It is not a vehicle inspection. No one has looked at, tested or driven your vehicle.
  • It is not a substitute for an in-person diagnosis by a qualified technician.
  • It is not a repair quote. Reports don't include parts prices or repair estimates.

3. For best results, upload your shop's scan

For best results, upload the diagnostic scan results from your local auto repair shop. A full scan usually shows more than a basic code reader: which computers were read, every stored and pending code, and the conditions when a code was set. Without a scan, a report relies on the codes and descriptions you type in, and it may be less specific.

4. Aims, not guarantees

The goal of every report is a clearer read of your codes, symptoms and scan, and a sensible order of next steps. We can't promise that a report will identify the exact cause or the exact fix. The same code can have several causes, and vehicles can have more than one problem at once. Next steps are suggestions to consider, listed easiest first; they are not instructions you must follow.

5. Safety comes first

  • If your vehicle has a safety issue, such as brakes, steering, airbags, a flashing check engine light, overheating, or a smell of fuel, don't wait on a report. Stop driving when it's safe to do so and have the vehicle checked in person.
  • Only take on work you have the tools, training and safe setup for. Follow your owner's manual and the manufacturer's safety warnings. Some systems (for example airbags, high-voltage ignition and hybrid or EV systems) can cause serious injury.
  • Recall information is listed by year, make and model. Always check your exact VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls to see whether your vehicle is included. Recall repairs are done by a dealer.

6. Sources and accuracy

Reports draw on public sources such as NHTSA's recall and complaint databases, owner's manuals and published repair information, and each item links to its source. We aim to include only information that can be backed up for your specific vehicle and to leave out anything that can't. Sources can be incomplete, out of date or wrong, and information can change after a report is written.

7. Nearby businesses

Mobile mechanics, parts stores and shops in a report are found near the city or ZIP you enter, at the time the report is written. Listings are not ads, paid placements or endorsements, and we don't vouch for any business's work, prices or availability. Please check them out yourself before you hire anyone.

8. Free test version

This site is a free test version. Features, wording and how reports are delivered may change as we learn from testers. You should receive your report typically within 10 minutes, though timing can vary while this is a test version. Replies to report emails aren't monitored.

9. Your decisions

You decide what to do with your vehicle. By requesting a report, you agree that you use the information at your own discretion and that you're responsible for any work you do or arrange.

10. Your information

We use what you send only to prepare and email your report. Your information is never sold to third parties or used to target ads.
